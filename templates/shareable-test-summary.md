# Shareable Test Summary

> Use this to tell another team, or anyone outside your company, what you tested and what happened, without sharing customer data. It describes the method and the result, never the material you used. Your full notes stay in your [Team Learning Log](team-learning-log.md), in an approved place.

## What We Tested

- **Team job:** the task in general terms, such as "drafting a follow-up after a discovery call". No customer, deal or product names.
- **AI tool and setup:** the tool, and whether you used a saved instruction, a template or a plain prompt.
- **Method:** what you asked the AI to do and what a person checked. Describe the steps; don't paste the prompt if it contains anything confidential.
- **How long and how often:** for example, "three people, two weeks, 14 follow-ups".

## What Happened

- **What helped:** in your own words.
- **What needed correcting:** the kinds of mistake, such as "invented a next step nobody agreed". Not the mistaken text itself.
- **Counts, if you kept them:** for example, "used with light edits 9 of 14 times". Only numbers you actually counted.
- **What we would change next time:**
- **Would we use it again?** Yes, no, or only for some jobs.

## What This Does Not Show

Say plainly what the test can't tell anyone. For example: "one team, two weeks, no comparison with doing it without AI".

## Before You Share

Check every item. If you can't tick one, fix it or don't share.

- [ ] No customer, prospect or employer names, and nothing that would let someone work out who they are (a small sector, a town, a deal size, a date).
- [ ] No pasted AI output or source material that contains customer information.
- [ ] No internal figures such as prices, margins, pipeline values or targets.
- [ ] Every number is one we counted, not an estimate or a hope.
- [ ] Anyone named in the summary has agreed to be named.
- [ ] Our company allows us to share this, and the person who approves that has seen it.

## What Kind Of Evidence Is This?

Label each thing you learned with the kind of evidence it is. Each row is stronger evidence than the one above it.

| Kind | What it shows | What it doesn't show | Ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use | People tried it | That anyone found it useful | How many people, doing which job? |
| Repeat use | People came back to it without being pushed | That the work is better | Who used it again, and when? |
| Quality | Someone checked the output against something and judged it | That the team works better overall | Who checked, against what, and what did they correct? |
| Result | A number the team already tracks has moved | That the AI caused it | What was it before, over a similar period, and what else changed? |

Counting logins or prompts is use. It isn't a result. An opinion that something saves time isn't a result either. Record it, and write it as an opinion.

# Northstar Customer Success First Month Review

> Fictional example. Northstar, its team and every outcome in this example are invented.

Northstar's customer success team had one approved AI tool, but people used it in disconnected ways. Some used it to tidy their own notes. Others avoided it because they worried it would make a confident sounding guess about an account.

The manager chose one low risk job: preparing a short internal account review brief from approved notes. The point was not to automate account management. It was to give the team a clearer starting point for an internal conversation.

## The Boundary

The team started with fictional account notes. During the following two weeks, four volunteers could use the method only with information approved for their existing tool.

They checked every statement against the source notes. They would not use the tool to assess account health, predict a renewal, contact a customer, change a CRM record or decide a commercial action.

## What Happened

| What the group found | What it meant |
| --- | --- |
| The brief made open actions and missing owners easier to see | The job was useful enough for another test |
| The first prompt treated a quiet customer as a possible risk too confidently | The wording needed to turn weak signals into questions to check |
| Three volunteers used the revised method again | There was some repeat use, not proof of wider adoption |
| One volunteer preferred a handwritten agenda for smaller accounts | The method stayed optional and situational |
| The final agenda gave weekly reviews a clearer shape | The output was helping a real part of the team's work |

## The Honest Review

**What the team could say:** A short first draft helped some volunteers organise an internal account review. Making uncertainty visible stopped the output being mistaken for account judgement.

**What the team could not say:** It had not proven that reviews were faster, that renewals would improve or that the team should use one method for every account.

## The Next Step

Northstar chose to keep testing the revised method for another two weeks. It added the [Account Review Brief](../shared-assets/account-review-brief/README.md) to its learning log as an optional method, with an owner to review it after a month.

This is a good first result. The team has a useful structure, a known failure mode and a clear boundary around the judgement that still belongs to the account owner.

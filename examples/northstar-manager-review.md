# Northstar Manager Review

> Fictional example. Northstar, its team and every outcome in this example are invented.

## What the Team Tried

Five volunteers tested two safe jobs using the approved AI tool:

1. turning approved call notes into a first draft follow up; and
2. preparing discovery questions from public company information and safe internal notes.

Every output was checked by the person using it. No messages were sent automatically and no CRM records were changed automatically.

## What Helped

The follow up method gave people a useful first structure when the notes were clear. It saved the blank page problem and made it easier to spot missing actions before sending.

The call preparation method was most useful when the prompt separated confirmed information from assumptions to test.

## What Needed Correction

Three volunteers found that rough notes led to generic drafts. Two volunteers noticed that early prompts made a next step sound agreed when it had only been discussed.

The team changed the method to require a short evidence section and a final human check before use.

## Manager Decision

The team will test the follow up method again with a clearer input format. It will not become a shared asset yet because only one type of call has been tested.

The discovery question method can be used by volunteers, but it needs a clearer safe input guide before it is shared more widely.

## What the Review Did Not Prove

- That AI saved a particular number of hours
- That the method improves conversion or customer experience
- That the whole sales team should use the same approach
- That another tool would produce the same result

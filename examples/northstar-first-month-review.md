# Northstar First Month Review

> Fictional example. Northstar, its team and its activity are invented.

Northstar has one approved AI tool. Six commercial team members said they had tried it, but only two used it more than once. The manager did not start with a tool comparison or a broad productivity target. She asked for volunteers to test one job: preparing a short first draft follow up from a fictional call summary.

## The Boundary

The team agreed to use fictional material in the first session. In the following two weeks, volunteers could use the method only with information approved for their existing tool. They would check every factual statement, every customer commitment and the final tone before sending anything.

They would not upload customer call recordings, use the tool to decide an offer or let it send emails.

## What Happened

| What the group found | What it meant |
| --- | --- |
| The first drafts gave people a useful structure | The job was worth another test |
| The drafts occasionally made weak notes sound more certain than they were | The prompt needed an explicit instruction to flag assumptions |
| Two volunteers used the revised method a second time | There was some repeat use, not proof of team wide adoption |
| One person still preferred their normal process | The team kept the method optional rather than presenting it as best practice |
| The manager could see where people were getting stuck | The next session could focus on safe input and checking |

## The Honest Review

**What the team could say:** A short, structured first draft helped some volunteers organise an ordinary follow up task. Adding an assumptions check made the output easier to review.

**What the team could not say:** It had not proven a productivity saving, improved conversion or a case for a wider rollout. It had only been tested by a few volunteers over a short period.

## The Next Step

Northstar chose to keep testing the revised method with the same boundary for another two weeks. The manager added it to the [Team Learning Log](../templates/team-learning-log.md), rather than calling it a company standard.

This is a good result. The team has a clearer method, a known limitation and a sensible next question.

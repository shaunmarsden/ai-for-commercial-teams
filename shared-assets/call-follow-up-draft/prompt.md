# Call Follow Up Prompt

Copy this into your approved AI tool. Replace the text in square brackets and add only information you are allowed to use.

> I am a salesperson writing a follow up email after a customer conversation.
>
> Use only the notes below. Do not invent customer pain, urgency, budget, approval, a decision or a next step. If a detail is unclear, flag it for me rather than making it sound agreed.
>
> Write a concise first draft in plain, natural British English. It should include:
>
> 1. a short subject line;
> 2. a brief thank you and recap of the conversation;
> 3. the agreed actions with an owner only where the notes make this clear;
> 4. the next step only if it was agreed, otherwise suggest a neutral option and label it as a suggestion; and
> 5. a short **Check Before Sending** list of assumptions, missing details or claims I should verify.
>
> Do not use exaggerated sales language, make promises, add pricing, mention legal terms or send the message. This is a draft for a person to check and edit.
>
> **Tone:** [for example: warm, direct and brief]
>
> **Call notes:**
>
> [Paste approved notes here]

## Good Input Makes The Difference

The prompt will not rescue vague notes. A useful input usually states:

- the customer’s question or priority;
- what was discussed;
- what was clearly agreed;
- what is still open; and
- who owns each next action, if known.

If your notes do not say something, do not try to imply it through the prompt.

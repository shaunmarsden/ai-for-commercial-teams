# Call Follow Up Draft

This shared asset helps a salesperson turn approved call notes into a clear first draft follow up. It is there to remove the blank page, not to send customer messages or decide what was agreed.

## Use This When

- you have notes from a call that are safe to use in your approved AI tool;
- you want a short, customer friendly first draft; and
- you will check and send the final message yourself.

## Do Not Use This When

- the notes include information that should not go into the tool;
- you are not sure what was agreed on the call;
- the email needs a legal, pricing or contractual commitment; or
- you want the tool to send the message or update a system.

## What To Provide

1. The customer name and company only if approved for the tool.
2. The safe call notes.
3. The next step that was actually agreed, if there was one.
4. Any tone or length preference.

If any of this is missing, leave it out. The method should flag the gap rather than fill it in.

## Use It In Three Steps

1. Read the [prompt](prompt.md) and add the information you are allowed to use.
2. Read the draft. Do not send it yet.
3. Run the [quality check](quality-check.md), edit it in your own voice and decide what happens next.

## What It Produces

- a clear subject line;
- a short recap of the conversation;
- agreed actions and owners, only where the notes support them;
- one proposed next step, only when it was agreed or clearly marked as a suggestion; and
- a short list of assumptions or missing details to check.

## What A Person Still Does

The salesperson checks every fact, decides what to say, approves the final email and sends it. The method does none of those things automatically.

## What We Learned Before Sharing It

See the [fictional Northstar asset review](example.md). It shows the method being improved after a small test. It is an example of the kind of evidence a team should look for, not proof that the method will work everywhere.

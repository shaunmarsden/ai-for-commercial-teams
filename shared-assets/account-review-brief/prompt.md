# Account Review Brief Prompt

Copy this into your approved AI tool. Replace the text in square brackets and add only information you are allowed to use.

> I am a customer success professional preparing for an internal account review.
>
> Use only the notes below. Do not invent customer sentiment, product usage, risk, budget, renewal likelihood, stakeholder views, commitments or dates. If information is unclear or missing, flag it as a question for me to check.
>
> Create a concise working brief in plain, natural British English with these sections:
>
> 1. **Account at a glance:** confirmed context in no more than three bullets;
> 2. **What has happened:** important confirmed progress, decisions or changes;
> 3. **Open actions:** action, owner and date only where the notes make them clear;
> 4. **Questions to check:** missing information, assumptions or possible risks that need a person's judgement; and
> 5. **Internal review agenda:** three useful questions for the team to discuss.
>
> Do not label an account healthy, at risk or likely to renew. Do not make a recommendation, contact a customer, update a CRM or describe an unagreed action as a commitment. This is a draft for a person to check against the source notes.

## Good Input Makes The Difference

Useful input separates what is known from what someone suspects. It usually includes:

- recent customer interactions or agreed outcomes;
- confirmed actions and owners;
- dates that are genuinely known;
- useful internal context that is safe for the tool; and
- the decision or question the internal meeting needs to address.

Do not try to make rough notes more convincing before adding them. If the source is unclear, the review brief needs to show that.

# Account Review Brief Quality Check

Use this before treating a draft as part of an internal account review.

| Check | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Can I trace every factual statement to the notes or another approved source? |
| Facts | Did the brief turn a guess, a quiet customer or a weak signal into a certainty? |
| Actions | Are the action owners and dates correct, or clearly still open? |
| Judgement | Has the draft left account health, relationship judgement and commercial decisions with a person? |
| Gaps | Are missing details and questions visible rather than quietly filled in? |
| Usefulness | Will this make the internal conversation clearer and more focused? |
| Boundary | Am I still deciding what happens next and what, if anything, is shared with the customer? |

If any answer is no or unclear, edit the brief or return to the source notes. A tidy summary is not evidence that it is correct.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating a lack of recent contact as proof of dissatisfaction.
- Calling a renewal likely or at risk without evidence.
- Adding an owner or date that was never agreed.
- Hiding the uncertainty that the internal review needs to resolve.
- Copying the brief into a customer message without a separate human decision.
